2. Transfer and consent checks

Where the land is agricultural or otherwise controlled, the parties must obtain Land Control Board consent before the transfer can be completed. The legal checklist page shows when this step applies.

3. Direct settlement and verification finality

By signing this document, the Buyer and Seller consent that the mutually agreed sum of KSH {{ transaction.agreed_price }} is settled directly between buyer and seller according to the platform verification milestones once the contract is signed, boundary beacons are verified, and title transfer documentation is executed. DigiLand does not hold customer funds in escrow.

4. Matrimonial property check

Where the specified property constitutes matrimonial property, the parties should verify the required written and informed spousal consent before final execution and registration.
