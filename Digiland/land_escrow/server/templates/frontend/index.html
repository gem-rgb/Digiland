How It Works

Three steps to a secure land transfer

From listing to final settlement, every stage is verified and protected.

01

List & Pre-Verify Property

Upload title deeds, mutation maps, and compliance files. Registry cross-referencing and due-diligence screening verify ownership before marketplace listing.

02

Licensed Professional Due Diligence

A registered Land Surveyor inspects on-site boundary beacons, and a conveyancing advocate clears spousal consent, rates, and Land Control Board approvals.

03

Direct Settlement & Transfer

Buyer settles payment directly to the seller with verified M-PESA or bank receipts. Zero middleman holding fees, zero delays, and full milestone audit transparency.

Platform Features

Everything you need for safe property deals

Whether you're buying, selling, surveying, or co-investing — Digiland has you covered.

Triple-Layer Due Diligence

Title deeds, ID documents, and registry searches are validated by registered surveyors and conveyancing advocates before closing.

Direct Non-Custodial Settlement

Settle directly to the seller via M-PESA or RTGS with automated cryptographic confirmation receipts. No escrow middleman holding your funds.

Licensed Land Surveyors

Board-registered surveyors visit the parcel, confirm physical corner beacons, verify GPS coordinates, and submit signed boundary reports.

Joint Ownership Syndicates

Co-purchase land with family, chamas, or investment partners. Automated equity calculations and separate member payment tracking.

Digital Conveyancing

Both parties sign LSK-standard legal contracts digitally. Every signature, mutation, and consent is timestamped for a tamper-proof audit trail.

Land Registry Sync

Parcel records are cross-referenced with Kenya's official Ministry of Lands registry to confirm genuine ownership before listing.
