The Escrow
Advantage

Funds are locked in a regulated vault and only released once the title is cryptographically transferred in your name.

How it works

1. Verify Core Title

We pull real-time government registries from ArdhiSasa, ensuring the parcel is conflict-free before you pay.

2. Lock Node Funds

Buyers deposit via M-PESA into a secure vault. Sellers see funds are ready — but can't access them until verified transfer.

3. Exchange Keys

Both parties sign electronically. The vault unlocks and atomic settlement executes instantly.
